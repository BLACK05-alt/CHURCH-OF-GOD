About us

Living the Gospel in everyday life

At COG San Juan, we believe that the Church is the living body of Christ, called to teach, worship, pray, and serve. We are committed to helping people know Jesus Christ, grow in spiritual maturity, and shine His light in the community.

We welcome families, youth, children, and every believer seeking a deeper walk with God. Through worship, fellowship, and biblical teaching, we nurture hope, holiness, and unity.

Our mission

To make disciples and glorify God

  • Teach the Word of God faithfully and clearly.
  • Build a loving and prayerful church family.
  • Reach the lost with the message of salvation.
  • Serve the community with compassion and generosity.
